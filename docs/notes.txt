power input, from left to right, looking into the pins are:
5V, ground, 12V, -12V, NC?, 5V, GND, 12V, -12V
pins are spaced 2.50mm.

ICs are labeled by their coordinate, number then letter (like "1J").
Everything(?) else is labeled conventionally, Q1 is the first transistor,
Y1 the first crystal, etc.

the cpu sram derives its power from the +12V rail, through Q2.
you can get the system to start by putting 5V onto that rail, and
it'll work. to see if you're having that problem and/or if you've
resolved it, check VCC on 1J, the 6264.

if the SRAM isn't working (profoundly failed, not powered), the first
read of SRAM will fail. that happens right after it tries to write to
the video ASIC twice. you can spot those on the video ASIC's pin
on the 74138 that decodes address space (2H). two low pulses, a delay
(i think it was 100ms) and then the pulses again, forever.

Y2 (12.288MHz crystal), Q3 (small BJT) and 6G (74LS390) generate the
CPU clock, the clock for all of the CTC CLK inputs, and presumably the
clock for the SIO baud rates, but i didn't track down that last one.

video asic also handles the keyboard. i can't figure out the electrical
path to save my life, though.
