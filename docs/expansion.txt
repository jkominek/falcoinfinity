/IOSel is Y1 on the 74138 that divides up IO address space.
The expansion port corresponds to 0x10 to 0x1F.

I don't see a way to participate in memory operations, but I
believe you could use every mirror of the IO space allocated
to the port. That would give you 8192 IO ports. You could also
snoop memory reads/writes.

Perhaps if you can turn off the video chip's VRAM aperture
(0xE000-0xFFFF), so that it doesn't attempt to drive the data
bus, then the expansion port could decode the region and
respond.
