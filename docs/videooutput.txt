Looking toward the pins of the video connector, they are, from left to right:
ground, hsync, ***, video, vsync, ground
pins are spaced 2.50mm.

the "***" pin isn't present in the cable, but i think on the pcb it
goes over towards the audio circuitry.

vsync and hsync are 5V active high logic level signals.

video is 50ohm output impedance. it ranges from 0V to 2.7V, with an
intermediate level of 2.05V or so in the front/back porch.  not yet
sure if the pixels are active high or low, the terminal might have
been in an inverted mode when i was looking at it.

70Hz vertical refresh, somewhere in the vicinity of 33kHz horizontal
refresh. i didn't measure it precisely.

output amplifier is a 2N2369A (Q1). input signal goes to the
base. collector is connected to 5V, emitter is connected to the output
pin through a 47 ohm resistor, and pulled to ground through a 270 ohm
resistor.
